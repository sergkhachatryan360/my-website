01 / A LITTLE ABOUT US

ALTERATIONS BY GEORGE

Good style starts
with the right fit.

Clothes should feel as good as they look. The right fit can bring balance to a silhouette, comfort to an everyday favorite and confidence to an outfit made for an important moment.

At Alterations By George, we help care for the pieces that make up your wardrobe. Whether you need a hem adjusted, a jacket refined, a dress prepared for a celebration or guidance with garment care, our goal is to give each item thoughtful attention.

We welcome everyday clothing as well as special pieces that deserve a little extra care. Visit us in Woodland Hills and speak with us about what your garment needs.
